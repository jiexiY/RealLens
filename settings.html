Voice and reading help

Use your device’s screen reader, such as VoiceOver or NVDA, to read labels, values, and expanded details. RealLens cannot turn on your device’s screen reader.

RealLens page introductions start when voice is on. Read page speaks the visible information and selected form values. Expand a section and choose Read details to hear it. Opening details alone does not start speech.

Use Stop reading or Escape to stop audio, or Turn voice off to mute all app speech. Browsers may require Play voice before sound can start. Microphone and location access require your action.
